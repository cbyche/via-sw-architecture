## Detailed disposition

### DP-00 runtime pilot, Profile Z, and R1-R4

What remains valid: run/source identity, canonical route and task events, legacy-contract reproducibility, framework-overhead observations, fixed-delay topology sensitivity, and known instrumentation/calibration failure modes.

What does not remain valid: legacy FTOL as QA-01, AECR as QA-02, model-call overhead as current QA-04, old aggregates, and old rankings as current selection evidence. Acoustic EOS exists, but the raw stream does not establish both required Voice facts audibly delivered and same-version Text details available. P01 is a state-changing volume operation and cannot establish the read-only `@` tactic.

Required rerun: adapted scenarios in the actual QA-v1 populations, using the current output/deadline/oracle requirements and Reference Environment v1.

### Historical QA-01 design reference

What remains valid: 36 semantic seed concepts, deterministic trace composition, Qwen evidence provenance, and latency sensitivity assumptions. Reference Environment v1 explicitly reuses the Medium profile source.

What does not remain valid: the simulated p95 values, Monte Carlo realization count as semantic population size, A0/B0 or C/B1/D1 rankings as QA-v1 results, or any production/device latency claim.

Required rerun: all adopted goals need QA-v1 Voice/Text delivery observations and inclusion in the frozen 150-goal population.

### Historical QA-03 evolution campaign

What remains valid: patches, implementation commits, acceptance/regression techniques, Agent-onboarding and other change concepts, and evidence about historical responsibility boundaries.

What does not remain valid: the five-case denominator, Expected Change Area oracle, or 60/60/80/80 CCR values as current QA-04 or QA-05. The current Agent Integration area and Z1-Z8 preauthorization were not frozen for those runs.

Required rerun: adapted cases within `qa04-agent-evolution-v1` or `qa05-product-evolution-v1`, with current ownership/seam declarations made before implementation.

### Analysis and DP records

AA-006 and the DP-00 executable spec are strong E4 descriptions. AA-021 and AA-026 are E0 interpretations. DP-13 through DP-16 have no measured alternatives; they contribute problem/ownership structure only.

QA-07's existing population identity is already `qa07-resource-v1`, and its metric, denominator meaning, target, and score bands need not change. The foundation therefore defines a concurrent event timeline rather than inventing a case count.

population. The corpus manifest freezes workload identity
`qa07-resource-workload-v1` beneath the unchanged population identity
`qa07-resource-v1`. No integer count was invented, and no QA metric,
denominator meaning, target, scoring band, or Reference Environment policy
changed.
